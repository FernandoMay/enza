02 / PURPOSE & PRODUCT

Holistic wellbeing, made accessible through everyday life.

ENZA brings together mindful indulgence, everyday wellbeing, conscious living and thoughtful gifting. Products, maker stories and accessible education help people make choices aligned with their needs and values.

Body & mind

Physical and mental wellbeing: nourishment, movement, rest, attention and learning.

Emotion & connection

Emotional and social wellbeing: reflection, meaningful relationships, community and reciprocity.

Meaning & environment

Spiritual and environmental wellbeing: purpose, personal rituals, care for our spaces and responsible consumption.

From philosophy to practice. Breath awareness, meditation, yoga, mindful eating and time in nature can inform accessible daily routines. These six dimensions are ENZA’s framework; “attunement” describes awareness and habit, rather than a proven quantum mechanism.

Commercial foundation: product sales, curated hampers and corporate gifting, with a broader multi-vendor marketplace as the next stage.

04 / WHY THIS NEXT STEP MATTERS

Help independent makers participate in connected commerce.

As the marketplace grows across geographies, each order can create multiple vendor balances, payout destinations and reconciliation tasks. ENZA’s proposed financial layer gives the operator a clear record of who is owed what and whether a payout has completed.

The value proposition combines ENZA’s curation and maker relationships with more transparent financial operations. The next validation is practical: measure payout cost, time, reconciliation effort and vendor usability against the current process.

For makers

Clear sales, fees, balances and payout status.

For ENZA

Order-level allocation and traceable settlement records.

For customers

A curated shopping experience grounded in useful product information.

What we must prove

That the selected payout route improves real vendor outcomes and operating costs.

05 / STELLAR WALLETS AS A PAYOUT SOLUTION

One order. Clear vendor shares. Verifiable payouts.

Stellar is the proposed settlement rail within ENZA’s financial workflow. The first integration connects a vendor’s verified wallet destination to a recorded order allocation and payout receipt.

01

Record

Capture the order, vendor, currency, fees and fulfilment status.

02

Allocate

Calculate vendor balances and platform commission using deterministic financial rules.

03

Pay out

Authorise a transfer of a supported asset to the vendor’s Stellar wallet.

04

Reconcile

Attach the network, asset, transaction hash and confirmed status to the payout record.

Wallet and asset decisions

Choose how vendors connect or receive a wallet, who controls signing, which asset is used, and how recipients can access its value. Explain balances, fees and recovery clearly.

Cross-border scope

Availability depends on the country, asset, provider, liquidity and local payout route. Network fees are one part of total cost; conversion and on/off-ramp fees also matter.

Planned integration. No wallets are connected and no payments are executed by this blueprint. Billing and tax workflows remain separate configured business processes; Stellar does not automatically calculate taxes or enable every currency.

06 / THE HACKATHON BUILD

Demonstrate the transition with one complete payout journey.

Build a focused testnet prototype that shows how ENZA’s existing commerce can connect to wallet-based vendor settlement.

COMMERCE

Create a sample order

Select catalogue items, associate each item with a vendor and show the total. Clearly label any sample product data.

FINANCIAL LOGIC

Calculate the allocation

Show vendor shares, platform fees and any excluded costs. Use integer minor units and verify that the allocation reconciles.

STELLAR TESTNET

Verify a wallet payout

Execute an authorised testnet transfer, display a verifiable transaction hash and update the payout status only after confirmation.

Demo definition of done: order → vendor allocation → wallet destination → authorised testnet payout → transaction evidence → reconciliation. Testnet assets have no real monetary value.

AI’s supporting role

Assist product discovery, explain order allocations and flag unusual records. Financial calculations and payment permissions belong to explicit code and authorised workflows.

After the prototype

Validate vendor onboarding, wallet usability, refunds, custody, provider coverage and total settlement cost before piloting a production payout route.
